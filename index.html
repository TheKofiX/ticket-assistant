Ticket Assistant

Summarize Freshdesk tickets and draft replies in seconds

A free Chrome extension for support agents. It works with your own Anthropic or OpenAI key.

Add to Chrome

Free. You pay the AI provider directly, typically $1–10 a month.

  • Catch up fast

    One click gives you a short summary of the ticket: the problem, what's been tried and what's still open.

  • Replies in your style

    Draft a reply in your tone, using your saved answers. You review and edit it, then paste it in yourself.

  • Private by design

    No server, no tracking. Ticket text goes only to the AI provider you choose, and only when you click.
