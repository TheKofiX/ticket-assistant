Ticket Assistant

Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what the Chrome extension "Ticket Assistant for Freshdesk" does with your data. The extension is free. You use it with your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI.

  • Everything you set up stays in your own browser.
  • Ticket text is sent only to the AI provider you chose, and only when you click a button.
  • There is no server, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your data.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

What is stored, and where

The extension stores the following in your browser's own storage (chrome.storage.local), on your computer only:

  • your API key, the AI provider you chose and the model name
  • your writing style: your name, sign-off line, tone and any example replies you add
  • your saved answers
  • a count of how many times Summarize and Draft reply have worked for you, and whether you've answered the "leave a review" request. This is only a number, with nothing about your tickets.

This storage isn't synced to your other devices or your Google account, and it's never sent to us. We don't have a server, so there's nowhere for it to go.

How ticket text is used

The extension reads the ticket you have open only when you click Summarize or Draft reply. It then sends the ticket's subject and messages (private notes are left out), plus your writing style, your saved answers and any instruction you typed, straight from your browser to the AI provider you chose, using your API key. The answer comes back to your browser and is shown in the side panel.

To show which ticket is open, the side panel also reads the ticket number and subject from the page. These stay in your browser and aren't sent anywhere.

When you click Test my key in the settings, your key is sent to the provider to check that it works. No ticket text is sent.

The extension never sends replies or changes anything in Freshdesk. It only shows a draft for you to review and paste in yourself.

Once the provider receives a request, it handles the data under its own privacy policy and terms:

  • Anthropic privacy policy
  • OpenAI privacy policy

What we don't do

  • There is no server. The extension talks only to Freshdesk (to read the page) and to your AI provider.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads and no cookies of our own.
  • We don't sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • We don't load code from the internet. All the extension's code ships with it.

What each permission is for

sidePanel
Shows the Ticket Assistant panel next to your Freshdesk tickets.
storage
Saves your key, settings, writing style and saved answers in your browser, as described above.
Access to https://*.freshdesk.com/*
Lets the extension read the ticket you have open, and turns the side panel on only on Freshdesk pages. It doesn't read any other website.
Access to https://api.anthropic.com/*
Sends your requests to Anthropic, if you chose Anthropic.
Access to https://api.openai.com/*
Sends your requests to OpenAI, if you chose OpenAI.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is used only to give you the summaries and drafts you ask for. It isn't transferred to anyone except the AI provider you chose, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

How to delete your data

Remove the extension. Open chrome://extensions, find Ticket Assistant and click Remove. Chrome then deletes everything the extension stored, including your API key.

To delete only your key, open the extension's settings and click Remove key. For anything already sent to Anthropic or OpenAI, see their privacy policies above.

Changes to this policy
